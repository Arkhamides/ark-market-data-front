Version 0.1
Date March 2026
Status Early Stage

Product Vision

A personal algorithmic trading simulation environment where you use AI to brainstorm, configure, and observe trading strategies against live market data — before risking real capital.

Core Loop

1
Observe Live aggregated order book and market data from Binance, Kraken, and Coinbase
2
Brainstorm Chat with AI (Claude via MCP) about strategy ideas and current market conditions
3
Configure Set alerts and strategy parameters through the AI interface
4
Simulate Paper trade in real-time against live data — no capital at risk
5
Evaluate Review P&L simulation, slippage estimates, fill stats, and strategy performance

Feature Roadmap

PHASE 1 Core Simulation
  • P&L simulation engine — paper trading against live data
  • Slippage estimation derived from live order book depth
  • Strategy configuration UI — entry/exit rules, position sizing
  • Stats panel — fill history, cumulative P&L curve, win rate
PHASE 2 Richer Market Data
  • Candlestick / price charts
  • Alert expansion: order book imbalance, spread change, arbitrage opportunities
  • Order book depth heatmap (liquidity visualization)
  • Trade flow visualization
PHASE 3 Multi-Instrument
  • Multi-instrument support (multiple pairs simultaneously)
  • Synthetic / spread pairs (e.g. BTC-ETH ratio as a derived series)
  • Pairs trading and statistical arbitrage strategy support
PHASE 4 AI Depth
  • Model routing — Haiku for low-latency alerts, Sonnet for strategy reasoning
  • AI-generated performance commentary and post-trade analysis
  • AI-suggested parameter tuning based on simulation results
