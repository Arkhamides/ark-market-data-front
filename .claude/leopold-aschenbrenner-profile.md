## Background

**Leopold Aschenbrenner** is a German-Austrian economist, AI safety researcher, and writer. He studied economics at **Columbia University** (BA, valedictorian, class of 2021), where he focused on macroeconomic growth theory and wrote on long-run economic forecasting. He's best known publicly for his tenure at OpenAI, his subsequent departure, and his landmark publication *Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead* (2024).

- Published academic work on **long-run growth and AI economics** during his Columbia studies

### 2. AI Safety Research
- Worked on **OpenAI's Superalignment team** (2023–2024) — the core technical safety group tasked with solving alignment for superintelligent systems
- Research focus: **scalable oversight**, interpretability, and evaluation of frontier models
- Departed OpenAI in April 2024, reportedly after raising internal safety concerns that became public

### 3. AGI Timeline Modeling & Forecasting

- Builds forecasts from first principles: compute scaling laws → capability jumps → economic/geopolitical implications
- Synthesizes ML research, economic theory, and historical analogues (Manhattan Project, industrial revolutions)
- Publicly defends specific numeric predictions, not just directional claims

### 4. Long-Form Research Synthesis
- Authored *Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead* — a **165-page essay** covering AI progress, national security, economic transformation, and governance
- Demonstrates ability to synthesize across ML research, economics, geopolitics, and policy into a single coherent argument
- Persuasive academic writing aimed at technically literate but non-specialist audiences

### 5. Geopolitical & National Security Analysis
- Deep analysis of **US–China AI competition** as a central strategic risk
- Advocates for treating AI development as a national security imperative, comparable to nuclear deterrence
- Engaged with policy circles, think tanks, and defense-adjacent institutions on AI governance

### 6. Capital Allocation & Investment Strategy (Forethought)
- After OpenAI, co-founded **Forethought** — an AI-focused hedge fund (public markets, not VC)
- Applies his AGI timeline thesis to investment strategy: bets on infrastructure that matters in a post-AGI world
- Bridges technical AI understanding with financial decision-making
- Backers include **Patrick Collison** (Stripe CEO) and other prominent tech figures

## Forethought — Operating Model

### Structure
- **Hedge fund** (not VC) — primarily bets in **public markets** via equities and derivatives
- Founded mid-2024, relatively small and concentrated — a personal macro fund, not a large institutional vehicle
- Backed by Patrick Collison and others from the tech world

### Investment Thesis
The fund is essentially a bet on his own essay:

1. **AGI arrives ~2027** → economic impact will be massive for infrastructure players, catastrophic for incumbents
2. **The compute stack is the trade** → companies providing physical/software infrastructure for frontier models are undervalued if AGI timelines are correct
3. **Public markets haven't priced in AGI** → alpha comes from acting on a timeline the market disbelieves
4. **Concentrated positions on AI infrastructure** → Nvidia, cloud compute, and the tooling layer around AI agents — not speculative startups

### Strategy Philosophy
- High-conviction macro positioning based on a specific timeline model
- Asymmetric risk profile: wrong = modest underperformance, right = massive upside
- Explicitly does not want a large diversified fund — concentration is the point
